Content Creation & Video Editing

I have hands-on experience in creating and editing comedy-based short-form videos for clients. My approach to content creation goes beyond basic video editing. I focus on understanding the concept, identifying entertaining moments, maintaining comedic timing, and transforming raw footage into engaging content.

I use creative storytelling, audience psychology, trending content formats, visual effects, sound design, and editing techniques to make videos more entertaining and relatable.

Working on client projects has helped me understand different content styles, creative requirements, and how to adapt my editing approach based on the target audience and platform.

My Creative Workflow

  1. 01
    Understanding the Concept

    Understanding the client's requirements, target audience, video purpose, and comedy style.

  2. 02
    Content Selection

    Reviewing raw footage and identifying the funniest, most engaging, and relevant moments.

  3. 03
    Creative Editing

    Structuring the video with engaging cuts, comedic timing, transitions, zoom effects, and storytelling.

  4. 04
    Sound & Visual Enhancement

    Adding sound effects, background music, subtitles, and visual elements to improve entertainment value.

  5. 05
    Final Optimization

    Reviewing the final video, refining the timing, and preparing it for Instagram Reels and other short-form platforms.
